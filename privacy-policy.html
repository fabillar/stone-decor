Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains, in plain language, what information Stone Decor collects through this website and when you contact us, and how we use and protect it.

01Who We Are

This website is operated by Stone Decor (“Stone Decor,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a natural stone and tile company with a showroom at 747 Miami Cir. NE, Atlanta, GA 30324. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

02Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact form, request a quote, sign up for updates, call, email, or visit our showroom, you may share:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Details about your project, such as the project type, materials, measurements, photos, or timeline
  • Anything else you choose to include in your message

Our contact and newsletter forms open your own email app with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send, and the message then reaches us by email like any other.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when pages are requested, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages visited, and the date and time of the visit. We use this only to keep the site secure and working properly.

03How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your questions and prepare quotes
  • Help with material selection, samples, showroom appointments, delivery, and installer referrals
  • Send design ideas, stone care tips, and updates if you asked to receive them
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website
  • Meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations

04How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to serve you:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as our website host and email provider, and only for that purpose
  • Installers, fabricators, or delivery partners, when you ask us for a referral or a delivery, and only the details they need for your project
  • Legal reasons, when required by law or to protect our rights, our customers, or others

05Cookies & Third-Party Services

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Some content loads from third parties, which may receive your IP address when your browser requests it:

  • Google Fonts, which supplies the fonts used on this site
  • Google Maps, which displays the map on our Showroom page and opens when you follow a directions link
  • Facebook and Instagram, when you follow links to our social pages

These services follow their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

06How Long We Keep Information

We keep project inquiries and customer records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet legal, tax, and warranty obligations. Newsletter contacts are kept until you unsubscribe. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

07Your Choices & Rights

You can contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct or update your information
  • Ask us to delete your information, unless we must keep it by law
  • Stop receiving our emails, using the unsubscribe link or by replying to any message

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. We will respond to requests within a reasonable time.

08Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. No website or email system is completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information, such as payment card numbers, through our forms or by email.

09Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for adults planning home and commercial projects. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted on our website.

11Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact Stone Decor at info@stonedecor.net, call (404) 848-9484, or write to us at 747 Miami Cir. NE, Atlanta, GA 30324.
